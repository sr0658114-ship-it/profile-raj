Planning South India tour packages the right way

South India covers five states, several climates and a genuinely wide range of landscapes, so choosing between South India holiday packages usually comes down to three questions: how much time you have, what pace you want, and which two or three regions to combine. A short weekend trip works best kept to a single hill station or coastal town close to your starting city. A longer multi-state South India travel package, on the other hand, is worth building around a natural pairing — hills into backwaters, or heritage into coast — rather than cramming in every well-known name.

Because road travel between regions in South India can take longer than distances suggest, we generally recommend no more than two major region changes in a single week-long trip. Beyond that, transit time starts eating into the days meant for actually being somewhere. Whether you are drawn to Kerala's backwaters, Karnataka's ruins, or a Tamil Nadu hill-and-coast combination, our travel booking support team can help sequence it properly before you commit to dates.

How long should a South India tour package be?

A 5 days South India tour package works best as a single-region trip: Ooty and Coonoor together, Munnar and Thekkady together, or Hampi with a night in Hospet. A 7 days South India tour package gives you room for one genuine region change — Kerala's hills into the backwaters is the most popular pairing. South India tour packages for 10 days are where multi-state routes start making sense, because you can cross from Tamil Nadu into Kerala or Karnataka without spending half the holiday in a vehicle. South India multi city tour packages beyond that are usually built around a specific interest: temple architecture, coastline, coffee country or wildlife.

Family, couple and budget options

South India family tour packages tend to need shorter driving days, flexible meal stops and rooms that can take an extra bed, so we lean towards hill stations and coastal towns with things to do within walking distance. South India tour packages for couples are usually the opposite — fewer stops, quieter properties and a houseboat or estate stay in the middle of the trip. If you are looking for affordable South India tour packages, weekend routes close to Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai or Bengaluru give you the most value, because the travel cost stays low and the whole budget goes into the destination.

What the packages include

Most South India tour packages with hotel and transport cover accommodation, inter-city transfers with a driver, and a planned day-by-day itinerary. Flights, train tickets, entry fees and meals outside the hotel are usually quoted separately so you can see exactly what you are paying for. Customized South India tour packages start from any of the routes on this page — tell us what to change and you will get a revised itinerary and price before anything is confirmed.

Planning independently as well? The official Kerala Tourism and Karnataka Tourism boards publish current seasonal and permit information that is worth checking alongside any itinerary. When you are ready, tell us what you have in mind and we will send a route and a starting price.

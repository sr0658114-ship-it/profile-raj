A South India tour and travel company that plans around you

Sutri Paar Tours and Travels is a South India tour and travel company built around a simple idea: a good holiday is designed, not assembled. We plan trips across Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana — hill stations, backwaters, coastline, wildlife and heritage towns — and we shape each route around how you actually like to travel rather than a fixed template.

Most travellers start by browsing our South India tour packages, then adjust the pace, the route or the number of days. Once the route is settled we match it with hotels and stays across the region, arrange transport between stops and hand over a day-by-day itinerary you can actually read. Travelling from Chennai, Madurai, Coimbatore or Bengaluru? Routes are usually built to start and finish wherever is easiest for you.

If you would rather talk it through first, our travel booking support team answers questions before you commit to anything, and you can send a tour enquiry whenever you are ready for a quote. There is more about Sutri Paar Tours and Travels on our company page, and you can see the trips we are running right now on Instagram — Follow Sutri Paar Tours and Travels on Instagram.

For independent destination background while you plan, the Government of India's Incredible India portal and the official Tamil Nadu Tourism site are both reliable starting points.

How It Works

Planning a trip with us, start to finish

Four steps from a rough idea to a finished itinerary. Nothing is payable until you have seen a plan you are happy with.

Step 01

Tell us the shape of the trip

Dates, how many of you are travelling, how long you have, and whether you are drawn to hills, coastline, backwaters, wildlife or heritage towns.

Step 02

We draft a route

You get a day-by-day outline with realistic drive times, suggested stays and the reasoning behind the order of stops — not just a list of place names.

Step 03

Change whatever you like

Swap a destination, slow the pace down, move to a different hotel category or extend a leg. Revisions happen before anything is confirmed.

Step 04

Travel with someone on call

You travel with contact details for each leg of the journey and travel booking support if plans need to shift mid-trip.

How to send a South India tour enquiry

Sending a South India tour booking enquiry usually saves more time than piecing a route together yourself, especially across multiple states. Start by sharing a rough shape — hills, beaches, backwaters or a mix — along with your dates and group size. From there, a planner drafts an itinerary, walks you through hotel options at your preferred budget, and adjusts anything that doesn't feel right before you commit.

What to include in your enquiry

The more specific your South India tour enquiry, the faster we can put together a useful draft. Useful details include your rough budget range, whether you prefer a relaxed or packed pace, any destinations you already know you want (or want to avoid), and any accessibility or dietary needs worth planning around.

Getting a quote for South India tour packages

A quote follows the enquiry rather than the other way round, because the price depends on the route, the season and the hotel category rather than a fixed per-head rate. Once you have seen a draft, you can ask us to rework it — shorter, longer, cheaper stays, fewer stops — and you will get a revised price before anything is confirmed. If the question is more about how booking, changes and cancellations work, our travel booking support page covers that in detail.
